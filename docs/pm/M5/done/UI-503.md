Evidence: at 1440 the band is `position: sticky; top: 0; z-index: 10` and measures exactly `0 … 32` at `scrollY` 0, 600 and 1530; the rail renders `0,32,176,314` (six 40px lanes, the 2px divider, two 36px reference rows) with six numbered lanes plus the two unnumbered reference rows, one `aria-current` and one roving tab stop; no `max-w-5xl` survives (computed `max-width: 64rem` count 0, one 1440px cap in the compiled CSS). At 390 the rail is a closed drawer that `MENU` opens, and `document.documentElement.scrollWidth === innerWidth` at 360, 390, 640, 768, 1023, 1024, 1280, 1440 and 1680. The drawer is fully keyboard-operable — focus lands on the active lane, arrows move and clamp, `Home`/`End` jump, `Space` activates, `Tab` stays inside — and `scrollY` is unchanged on open, while open and after close, with the `wheel`/`touchmove` guard bound on `document` for the open interval only. That guard replaced a dialog-bound one after measurement showed it inert: a listener on a `display:none` dialog forms no scroll-blocking region, so the page behind moved. Switching `prefers-color-scheme` moves values and no geometry.
