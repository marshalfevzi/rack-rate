# UI-506 closes with its scope narrowed: the 1px mobile head offset and the >=1280 readout column are withdrawn

## Context

The third pass authorized by DEC-2026-09-17-011 clause (d) shipped the table-anchored mechanism that decision mandated: a `<colgroup>` on each table (six `<col>`s on the readout listing, eight on `.console-table--index`), the cell-level `tr > :nth-child(n)` width rules deleted, the readout gutter kept as a real cell at zero width below 480px so the track count never changes, and the mobile head hidden with `position: static; visibility: collapse; height: 0` in place of the `display: block` and `position: absolute` pair that had voided the first row. A rebuilt preview measured ten of eleven criteria green in both schemes at 360, 390, 480, 600, 768 and 1440, with the readout's used tracks `[20, 0, 92, 88, 56, 56]` at 360, `[20, 28, 184, 88, 56, 56]` at 480 and `[20, 40, 244, 160, 120, 120]` at 768, row content heights exactly 44px below 768px and exactly 36px at 768 and 1440, and the equal-division regression signature (312/5, 342/5, 432/6, 552/6) absent at every width. `bun run check`, `bun test`, `bun run build` and `bun run data:check` exited 0 with `data/derived.json` still at `7425a331008fe0a1281a6d4f0bf4f350987f656cd135141a1ac69ef3f2317348`, and the impeccable detector returned no output for the three changed paths.

Two items stood outside the task's own criteria. First, the orchestrator's probe for Chromium's partial `visibility: collapse` support: with the mobile head collapsed, the first body row begins 1px below the table top at 360, 390, 480 and 600 in both schemes in all three tables, and removing the `thead` at runtime closes the gap to 0px. No document in this repository names a top-of-table offset, every frozen metric passes, and the 1px is a collapsed-boundary remnant of the same class DEC-2026-09-17-010 clause (d) already classifies as not a track defect.

Second, and material rather than cosmetic: the readout listing's box at and above the 1280px boundary is 464px wide, derived independently of the browser from the committed layout. At 1440px the workspace is 1168px (1440 less the 176px lane rail and two 48px edge gutters); the split console's selection track is `minmax(16rem, 22.5rem)` and takes its 360px maximum with a 32px gap, leaving 776px for the readout column; inside that column the set-aside track is `minmax(15rem, 18rem)` and takes its 288px maximum with a 24px gap, leaving 464px for the table. The pinned tracks total 460px — state 20, gutter 40, model 160, numerics 120 and 120 — so the one flexible column, `Plan`, measures exactly 4px and the committed plan name and provider render clipped at every width at or above that boundary. The verifier's independently measured tracks sum to 464px, matching that derivation to the pixel, while the same measurement reads 244px for `Plan` at 768px where the listing spans the full content width. No frozen metric catches it: DESIGN.md's 72px minimum governs the numeric columns at 768px and above, and no document pins a minimum for the flexible column.

So `/` at desktop does not show the committed plan name, which is a failure of the task's own Done-when clause 1 — '`/` with no query shows a committed plan rather than a placeholder' — at those widths. The owner narrowed UI-506's scope in the conversation on 2026-09-18 rather than spend a further fix round on geometry the site's own design overhaul will revisit.

## Decision

a. The 1px mobile top-of-table offset is withdrawn from UI-506's acceptance criteria. It is not a UI-506 defect; it is recorded in the task's `## Session` so it is not lost, and it may be swept by the design overhaul.

b. The readout listing's flexible `Plan` column at and above the 1280px boundary is withdrawn from UI-506's acceptance criteria and carried into the design overhaul. UI-506 therefore closes with `/` showing a clipped plan name at widths at or above 1280px. That is a recorded known gap, not an accepted permanent state, and it is named here rather than hidden: the readout listing's box is 464px against 460px of pinned tracks, so the fix is a geometry call on the split console — rebalancing the readout's desktop pins, the selection column's 22.5rem cap, or the set-aside's 18rem cap — which belongs to the overhaul rather than to a further UI-506 pass.

c. UI-506's remaining acceptance criteria are met and the task may finish. The four clauses of its `## Scope` Done-when hold at the widths below the 1280px boundary, including the no-query committed default, the `plan`/`models`/`tasks` URL round trip, the five former insight statements as rows, and the absence of any `.json` or API request; the stage acceptance in `docs/pm/M5/README.md` holds; and every frozen metric of DEC-2026-09-17-009, -010 and -011 measures green in both schemes at every viewport of the matrix.

d. The fix-round budget stays spent. No second fix round is granted by this record and none is needed for closure, because the two withdrawn items are excluded from the criteria rather than deferred within them.

e. Nothing else moves. No token, hue, radius or duration is added; `DESIGN.md` is unchanged; the frozen ten tokens, the 2px head rule, the sticky head at 32px, the never-dropped 20px state cell and the 36px/44px row tracks stand; and the mechanism shipped by this pass — table-anchored `<colgroup>` tracks with exactly one flexible column per table, and the gutter as a constant track so no row's cells ever shift a column left — is the durable outcome of the three decisions and is not reopened.

## Consequences

UI-506 moves from `docs/pm/M5/todo/` to `docs/pm/M5/done/` with `status: done` and `completed: 2026-09-18`, and M5's queue advances to UI-507 with UI-508 through UI-517 unchanged behind it. The task's `## Session` carries the measured matrix, the withdrawn 1px offset and the carried desktop column, so the next reader has the evidence without re-deriving it.

The carried defect must be filed when the design-overhaul milestone exists, naming the readout listing's >=1280 column geometry — a 464px box against 460px of pinned tracks, a 4px flexible `Plan` track — together with whichever of the three caps the overhaul moves. It is not filed as a task by this record; `/pm-new-task` against M5 can capture it in the meantime if the queue wants it tracked before then. The 1px mobile offset needs no task.

The stage gate is unchanged and was re-run green at closure: `bun run check`, `bun test` (165 pass, 0 fail), `bun run build` (53 pages) and `bun run data:check` all exit 0, with `data/derived.json` still at `7425a331008fe0a1281a6d4f0bf4f350987f656cd135141a1ac69ef3f2317348`.

Process note for the next task of this shape: the mobile track failures that consumed two earlier passes both traced to one unmeasured assumption — that `table-layout: fixed` reads its tracks from a first row that a later media query had taken out of the table's structure. The three decisions now pin the mechanism and the measurement obligation, so a future listing restyle reads DEC-2026-09-17-011 rather than rediscovering the arithmetic.
