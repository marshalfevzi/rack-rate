# The listing tables pin their own columns, and the phone form differs per table

## Context

Two `pm-ui-verifier` passes returned `fix` for UI-506. The route is already modular and gate-green; the failure is confined to the console listing CSS.

Reproduced on the built preview: route row 173px at 360, 68.5px at 390 and 480, 48.5px at 600, 36.5px at 768 and 1440 (required 36px at ≥768, 44px below 768); state cell 68.4px at 390, 72px at 480, 92px at 600, 30.6px at 768 (required 20px, never dropped); line gutter 72px at 480, 92px at 600, 61.2px at 768 (required 40px at ≥768, 28px at 480–767, absent below 480).

A fix round then introduced a regression: below 768px each of the five `HEADLINE INDEX` rows holds a fixed 44px track against 220px of content, so label, value, basis, confidence and source text overlap.

Root cause, in the unlayered console block of `apps/site/src/styles/global.css`: `table-layout: fixed` on a full-width table distributes the leftover width proportionally across every column, so a cell-level `width` cannot pin a column — the measured scaling factor is identical for the state cell and the gutter, 1.53 at 768px and 1.0085 at 1440px; and the blanket rule `.console-table tbody tr, .console-table tbody tr > td { height: 36px }` also applies inside the `.console-table--index` mobile grid, holding each cell at 36px against taller content.

The documents left the phone form open, and that is why UI-506 stopped in `todo/` with no commit: the `/` brief says 390px keeps 44px rows with the state mark inline before the plan name, while the shared shell drop order says rows stack as labelled records at 360px. The shipped CSS carries both readings — a `@media (max-width: 360px)` grid branch that stacks the readout listing beside the `.console-table--index` stacked layout — and the measured failures above are that unresolved choice, not a coding slip.

## Decision

The column width is authoritative, and the phone form differs by table. UI-506's remaining acceptance is unchanged and stays its own; this record settles the layout mechanism and the phone form only.

a. A column width is authoritative. The proportional-distribution layout is replaced by a mechanism that holds an explicit width per column — `table-layout: auto` plus `min-width`/`width` per column, a `<colgroup>`, or a grid — and the widths are pinned per table, so whatever the readout listing does cannot rescale the two headline index tables.

b. The phone form differs by table. The readout listing (`#console-table`, or a modifier class scope) keeps a single-row form at exactly 44px below 768px, with the state mark inline before the plan name, the line-number gutter absent below 480px, and all four fields — Plan, Model priced, Effective monthly cost, Break-even tasks/month — present and readable without hover and without horizontal scroll; truncation is allowed. The `≤360px` grid branch that currently stacks the readout listing as a labelled record goes with it. The headline index and headline figures tables (`.console-table--index`) keep the stacked labelled-record form below 768px, where a row's height is its content's height, every `data-label` identifies its field, and no fixed track may clip or overlap text.

c. Neither table may scroll horizontally at 360px, and the 2px head rule, the sticky head at 32px, the 20px state cell and the no-radius/no-shadow/no-gradient/no-blur/no-glow rules are unchanged.
