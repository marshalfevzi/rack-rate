# Reopen the Console Listing world, open M9 for the frontend rebuild, and represent cross-milestone prerequisites in task Notes

## Context

On 2026-09-18 the owner rejected the incumbent visual direction and the shipped content in one brief: "The current UI is fucking ugly. All context are gibberish, it doesnt give a proper insight. All frontend files are cluttered, there is no proper hierarchy and component structure. We cant continue like that. Also, there are no graphs." Asked to be specific about the content, the owner quoted the Overview's "Split Console" and "Headline Index" naming and the Method page's `budget: Committed field:quota_usd_month, tasks = quota_usd_month / api_cost_per_task_usd` line, and said a reader should understand the page quickly.

Two of the four complaints are defects the evidence supports. No chart draws at all: `apps/site/src/lib/charts/theme.ts` still reads the four colour tokens `UI-501` deleted, `resolvedToken` throws on an empty value, and every chart mount therefore fails before it reaches the renderer, printing `The chart could not be drawn; the static description remains available.` on all six plates. And the frontend is measurably cluttered: `apps/site/src/pages/compare.astro` is 711 lines with its own table engine, sort logic and payload assembly, `apps/site/src/lib/console-data.ts` is 514, `apps/site/src/lib/console-client.ts` is 394, `apps/site/src/pages/method.astro` is 386, `apps/site/src/pages/explore.astro` is 367 and `apps/site/src/pages/models/index.astro` is 326; the chart-plate markup is repeated six times across four section components and two routes; `Chip.astro` and `CostBasisChip.astro` carry the same style twice; and the plan helpers are duplicated between the two plan routes.

The other two complaints are direction. `DESIGN.md` locks the Console Listing world, and the owner reopened it rather than asking for it to be finished.

`/pm-align`-style restructuring of this size has no command surface that can express it in one step, and the plan validator holds one hard constraint that shaped the outcome: `pre-order` requires every prerequisite to appear earlier in its own milestone's task order, so a prerequisite residing in another milestone is not representable in `pre` at all.

## Decision

1. **The Console Listing is rejected as the visual direction.** It is replaced rather than amended: `DESIGN.md` is re-derived from a chosen world by `UI-901`, which renders three candidate worlds as specimens from a built preview and has the owner choose one before any downstream task is written. The two rejected directions are recorded as anti-references.

2. **`M9` opens to own the replacement**, titled "Frontend rebuild — a world that reads, a structure that holds": `UI-901` chooses the world, `UI-902` rebuilds the design system from it (tokens, type, rhythm, shell, primitives, chart grammar), `APP-904` flattens the frontend onto one documented composition convention, and `UI-903` replaces the internal vocabulary and field-level formulas with a plain answer per route under a closed vocabulary list.

3. **Eight route-composition tasks move from M5 to M9** with their dependency chains: `UI-507`, `UI-508`, `UI-510`, `UI-511`, `UI-512`, `UI-514`, `UI-515` and `UI-517`. Each composed a route into the world that is being replaced, so leaving them in M5 would have built five routes twice.

4. **M5 keeps only what outlives the world it was found in**: `UI-509` (no chart draws), `UI-513` (`/method` prints field-level formulas at the reader) and `APP-516` (the social-card renderer changes and no visual world). M5 closes by retro once those three are done.

5. **Cross-milestone prerequisites are carried in the task's `pre` as the same-milestone subset, with the full set recorded in the task's `## Notes`.** The validator cannot express an edge that leaves its milestone: restoring `UI-514`'s or `UI-517`'s full authored `pre` in M9 fails `pre-order` with `Prerequisite UI-509 must appear before UI-514`. The owner was offered the alternative — moving `UI-509`, `UI-513` and `APP-516` into M9 as well, which restores every authored edge verbatim — and chose on 2026-09-18 to keep the split. The effective ordering is unchanged, because M5 is the `in_progress` milestone and its queue is consumed before M9's `backlog` queue; the two task bodies name the removed edges and the reason.

## Consequences

M5's Definition of done, its eleven-route acceptance text and its stage record are now read against a reduced scope, and its `## Scope change — 2026-09-18` section records what moved and why; the eleven-route claim and the frozen `data/derived.json` pin are inherited by `docs/pm/M9/README.md`. `UI-509` and `UI-513` remain restyles of the outgoing world, so the display work they describe may be re-derived by `UI-902` and `UI-903` while the defects they fix are fixed in M5 rather than deferred. `M8`'s `DATA-801` and `DATA-802` stay gated on M5's hash pin until M5 closes, since both re-run `compute`. `UI-901` is the gate for the whole of M9: no downstream task may be written before the world is chosen, and `DESIGN.md` remains the only owner of token values so no route may introduce a local override. The `pre` representation is a schema limitation, not a weakened dependency, and it is revisited by `/pm-align` if the validator ever gains cross-milestone edges.
