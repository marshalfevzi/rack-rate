# The scheme follows `prefers-color-scheme` only; there is no toggle

## Context

From the Stage 3.3 + 3.3b session's owner decisions:

- The focus ring is ink, not an accent, so no colour role is spent on
  interaction chrome.

## Decision

The light scheme is `prefers-color-scheme` only: no toggle, no pre-paint
script, no FOUC workaround. A toggle needs persisted client state, which task
5.1 owns.

## Consequences

The toggle is still unbuilt and needs persisted client state. The redesign's
UI-501 ships the value-only inversion, and stage 5 surfaces may not imply a
saved preference; `docs/pm/M5/README.md` records that boundary.
