# The listing tracks are exact, and the phone readout's four fields outrank the 72px numeric minimum

## Context

The pm-ui-verifier pass on UI-506 rebuilt the tree and measured the built preview at 360, 390, 480, 600, 768 and 1440 in both schemes. Three findings are unambiguous. First, `table-layout: auto` with cell-level `width`/`min-width`/`max-width` does not hold the frozen tracks: at 768px the state cell rendered 28.6875px and the gutter 57.390625px against a required 20px and 40px, while at 1440px both were exact, so the used width is content-derived at the tightest desktop width. Second, a row grows past its track whenever a cell needs more height: the readout listing rendered 40.5px at 1440px, and the headline index and headline figures rows rendered 41-61px at 1440px and 40.5-134px at 768px, against a required 36px. Third, at 360px the document measured 391px of scrollWidth inside a 360px viewport in both schemes, a min-content floor that the phone form must not create. A fourth measurement, numeric columns rendering 41.703125px at 360/390, 44.3125px at 480 and 66.484375px at 600 against DESIGN.md's 72px numeric-column minimum, exposes a conflict the documents never faced: DEC-2026-09-17-009 clause (b) requires all four readout fields present and readable below 768px with no horizontal scroll, and at 360px the four fields plus a 20px state cell cannot also give two numeric columns 72px each inside a 312px content width. DEC-2026-09-17-009 settled the phone form but left the column mechanism open among three options and did not decide this conflict.

## Decision

a. A row track is exact and content may not grow it. Every listing row's used height is exactly 36px at 768px and above, and exactly 44px below 768px, in the readout listing and in the headline index and headline figures tables alike. At 768px and above every listing cell is single-line and truncates rather than wraps (`overflow: hidden`, `text-overflow: ellipsis`, `white-space: nowrap`) so that content can never raise the track. Below 768px the readout listing keeps the 44px single-row form with the same truncation, while `.console-table--index` keeps its stacked labelled-record form in which a row's height is its content's height and no track may clip or overlap a field.

b. A column's used width is authoritative, not content-derived. The mechanism must yield a measured used width equal to the specified width at every viewport in the matrix, so the accepted mechanisms are `table-layout: fixed` with every column pinned through `tr:first-child` child selectors or a `colgroup` and at most one flexible column, or an equivalent whose measured width is exact. `table-layout: auto` plus cell-level `width`/`min-width`/`max-width` is measured as not holding and is withdrawn from the options DEC-2026-09-17-009 listed. Cell-level width utilities that compete with the console block (`w-36`, `min-w-[72px]`) must be overridden from the unlayered console block, which outranks `@layer utilities` in the Tailwind V4 cascade, rather than left to win.

c. On the phone readout the four fields outrank the numeric minimum. DESIGN.md's 72px numeric-column minimum is a 768px-and-above rule. Below 768px the readout listing keeps Plan, Model priced, Effective monthly cost and Break-even tasks/month all present and readable without hover and without horizontal scroll, and its numeric columns take the width the four fields and the 20px state cell leave, truncating text values rather than producing scroll. `document.documentElement.scrollWidth === document.documentElement.clientWidth` holds at 360px. The head rule, the sticky head at 32px, the never-dropped 20px state cell, the 40px gutter at 768px and above, the 28px gutter at 480-767px, its absence below 480px, and the no-radius/no-shadow/no-gradient/no-blur/no-glow rules are unchanged.

d. A row's height is measured as its content height. A `<tr>` bounding rect under `border-collapse: collapse` includes half of the shared 1px rule, so the +0.5px offset observed at 360-768px is that collapsed border and is not a track defect; the 40.5px readout row at 1440px and the 41-61px index rows are content-driven and are defects.
