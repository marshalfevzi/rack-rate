## Scope

**Make every route say what it means.** The owner's complaint is specific and quotable: on the Overview the page is titled around a "Split Console" and a "Headline Index", and on Method a reader meets `budget: Committed field:quota_usd_month, tasks = quota_usd_month / api_cost_per_task_usd`. Both are internal names for things the reader was never told about. A reader arriving from a search result must understand the page in one glance, without knowing this repository.

**Files:** `apps/site/src/pages/` (every route's rendered copy); `apps/site/src/components/` (section heads, labels, column heads, empty and error strings); `apps/site/src/lib/provenance.ts` and `apps/site/src/lib/readout.ts` (the shared copy constants); `PRODUCT.md` (the vocabulary and answer-line contract); `docs/design/surfaces.md` (where it fixes copy).

**Work:** write the closed vocabulary list first — every internal or design-world term that must never reach the rendered document: the world's own name, "split console", "headline index", "readout", "lane", "carriage control", "listing paper", "set-aside", "provenance" used as a noun for a data fact, and the state-glyph letters as bare words. Record it in `PRODUCT.md` with the plain replacement for each. Then rewrite the rendered copy route by route against it.

**Each route leads with its answer.** The first viewport states, in one sentence a non-specialist can read, what the page answers — "the cheapest way to run these models", "which plan is worth buying at 300 tasks a month", "how every number on this site is computed" — followed by the specific finding the committed data supports, with its basis, confidence and source in the same block. A route that has no answer to give says so plainly instead of showing an empty frame.

**A formula is stated before it is written.** Where the site publishes arithmetic, Method states it first in words — what is being counted, in what unit, over what period, and what the result means for a purchase decision — and only then prints the exact field names and expression, because the field names are part of the evidence. The expression is never the explanation.

**The answer lines are derived, not written by hand.** Each route's opening finding comes from the committed payload through a pure helper, so it cannot drift from the table below it, and a missing input prints as a gap with its reason rather than as a zero.

## Done when

- No term from the closed vocabulary list appears anywhere in the rendered HTML of any of the eleven routes.
- Every route's first viewport carries a plain-language statement of what the page answers and the finding the data supports, with basis, confidence and source.
- Every published formula is introduced by a sentence in words before the field names appear, and no route leads with a field-name expression.
- Every answer line is produced by a pure helper over the committed payload, and a throwaway check proves it changes when the payload changes.
- `PRODUCT.md` carries the closed vocabulary list and the answer-line contract.
